Using Bhala

What does Bhala mean?

Bhala means “write” in isiZulu and isiXhosa. Bhala umsebenzi wakho: write your work, every day, and your logbook is done when you need it.

How long does a day take to log?

About two minutes. Bhala asks five short questions: what you worked on, the tools, the materials, how hands-on you were, and a photo if you have one.

Do I need data on site?

No. Once you have opened Bhala and added it to your home screen, it works offline. It only needs a connection to check your subscription now and then.

How do I put Bhala on my home screen?

Android (Chrome): open the app, tap the ⋮ menu, then Install app or Add to Home screen. iPhone (Safari): tap Share, then Add to Home Screen.

Can I fill in days I missed?

Yes, for the last 7 days. Bhala is built for logging as you go, so older days can’t be added later. This keeps your logbook honest and complete, and it’s what your trainer and assessor want to see. You can still fix any entry you already logged.

Can I get a daily reminder?

Yes. On the Me screen, pick a time and tap Add reminder. Bhala adds a reminder to your phone’s calendar for every work day.

Which trades does Bhala support?

Electrician, plumber, welder, boilermaker, carpenter, millwright, mechanical fitter, diesel mechanic, automotive mechanic, auto electrician and solar PV technician.

Your logbook and codes

Will my college accept the printout?

The PDF follows a logbook layout: a statement of work experience, your code progress, one page per week with tasks, tools, materials, codes and hours, and sign-off lines for you, your mentor and your facilitator. Each college sets its own rules, so check with your facilitator that a printed logbook is fine.

How are codes matched?

You pick the part of the job you worked on and how hands-on you were. Bhala turns that into your logbook code, for example 671101000-WM-02-WE02, and counts entries per code so you can see what still needs work.

Are the codes official?

The electrician codes follow the QCTO Occupational Certificate: Electrician (671101000). Codes for the other trades are being checked with trainers in each trade before we promote them to colleges.

What if I lose my phone?

Your logbook is saved on your phone, so back it up every month on the Me screen and keep the file somewhere safe, like Google Drive or email. Restore it on a new phone and your subscription comes with it.

Paying and cancelling

How much does Bhala cost?

Your first 30 days are free. After that it is R29 a month for apprentices. Colleges and employers can buy a licence for a whole class.

How do I pay?

Through PayFast’s secure page, by card. PayFast takes R29 each month until you cancel. Bhala never sees your card details.

How do I cancel?

Use the cancel link in the email PayFast sends you, or WhatsApp 060 580 5925 or email bhala.logbook@gmail.com. You keep access until the end of the month you paid for, and you keep everything you logged.

What happens when my free month ends?

Your logged days stay safe. You can still open, edit and back them up. You need a subscription to log new days and download your PDF.

Privacy

Who can see my logbook?

Only you. Your entries, photos and details are saved on your own phone. We don’t receive them.

What do you keep about me?

If you subscribe: your first name, email address, a random app number and your payment dates. That’s all. See the privacy page.
