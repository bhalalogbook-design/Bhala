The problem

No more rewriting months of logbooks before the trade test.

Trainers told us apprentices can spend three days rewriting entries and looking up codes before their trade test. Bhala lets them write each day as it happens, so the logbook is ready when they are.

Without Bhala

  • Notes on scraps of paper, or nothing at all
  • Months of entries rewritten by hand at the end
  • Codes looked up and guessed afterwards
  • No proof of the work you actually did

With Bhala

How it works

Three steps. Every work day.

  1. 1

    Log the day

    Answer five quick questions: the job, the tools, the materials, how hands-on you were, and a photo. Mostly taps, very little typing.

  2. 2

    Codes matched for you

    Bhala turns your answers into the right logbook code, like WM-02 · WE02, and shows which codes still need entries.

  3. 3

    Print your logbook

    Pick the dates and download a clean PDF, ready for your mentor to sign and your facilitator to check.

Features

Built for site, not for an office.

Big buttons, works without signal, and it speaks the logbook’s language.

Daily reminder

A reminder in your phone’s calendar every work day, at the time you choose.

Guided questions

Job, tools and materials, with suggestions for your trade. Tap instead of type.

Photos as evidence

Add a photo of the work. It goes into your printed logbook as proof.

Codes and levels

Every day matched to a work module and level: observe and assist, under direct supervision, or on your own.

Works offline

No signal in the roof or the plant room? Log anyway. Nothing is lost.

Private by design

Mentor sign-off

Weekly sign-off lines for apprentice, mentor and facilitator, and signed days marked in the app.

Backup and restore

One file holds your whole logbook. Move to a new phone in a minute.

The printout

A logbook your mentor can sign.

Your PDF is laid out like the logbook you would have written by hand, only neat, complete and quick.

Questions

Good to know

What does Bhala mean?

Bhala means “write” in isiZulu and isiXhosa. Bhala umsebenzi wakho: write your work, every day, and your logbook is done when you need it.

Do I need data on site?

No. Once you have opened Bhala and added it to your home screen, it works offline. It only needs a connection to check your subscription now and then.

Will my college accept the printout?

The PDF follows a logbook layout: a statement of work experience, your code progress, one page per week with tasks, tools, materials, codes and hours, and sign-off lines for you, your mentor and your facilitator. Each college sets its own rules, so check with your facilitator that a printed logbook is fine.

How do I cancel?

Use the cancel link in the email PayFast sends you, or WhatsApp 060 580 5925 or email bhala.logbook@gmail.com. You keep access until the end of the month you paid for, and you keep everything you logged.
